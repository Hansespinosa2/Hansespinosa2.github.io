A wonderful central hypothesis comprehensively analyzed and backed up with different pieces of evidence and anecdotes.
Passion is the spice of life.
No thing is as satisfying and fulfilling as dedicating yourself to something so fully that you care little about the outcome because the journey is so rewarding.
With a little luck and much passion, that outcome of success provides a life worth having been lived.
