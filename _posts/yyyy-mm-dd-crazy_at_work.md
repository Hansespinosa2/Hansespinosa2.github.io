"It Doesn't Have to Be Crazy at Work" is a rejection of the chaos worshipped by hustle culture in the modern workplace. Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH), founders of Basecamp, argue that a calm, focused work environment leads to better productivity and employee satisfaction. They don’t just question hustle culture—they put it on blast. Meetings? Mostly useless. Deadlines? Rarely real. Slack? A digital anxiety machine. They walk through the practices Basecamp lives by—what’s stuck, what’s been scrapped, and how they’ve built a "calm" company.

# Crazy at Work: A Reflection and Review
